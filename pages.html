Our Story

PDFMagic was founded in 2025 by a team of researchers and engineers who were frustrated by how much time they spent reading long documents — research papers, legal contracts, annual reports.

We built the first version in a weekend: a simple tool that uploaded a PDF and returned a summary. Within a month, 10,000 people were using it. Within three months, 50,000.

Today, PDFMagic is the world's fastest-growing AI PDF summarization platform, trusted by students, researchers, lawyers, and business professionals in 120+ countries.

Our mission is simple: help people understand more, faster, in any language.

PDFMagic AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use PDFMagic.io and our related services.

1. What Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash). We never store plain-text passwords.

Usage Data

We collect information about how you use our service, including pages visited, features used, summary types selected, and general performance metrics. This data is anonymized and used to improve the service.

Payment Information

All payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full credit card number. We receive only a payment confirmation token from Stripe.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the PDFMagic service
  • To send important account and billing notifications
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve performance
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising targeting. We do not share your data with data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored on secure servers in India and the European Union, encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. All data transfers are protected by TLS 1.3 encryption. We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing.

4. Your Files & Documents

This is our most important privacy commitment:

  • PDF files you upload are processed in memory and automatically deleted within 1 hour
  • Your documents are never stored permanently on our servers
  • Your documents are never used to train AI models
  • No human employee reads your uploaded documents
  • Document content is transmitted to the AI provider (Anthropic) only for processing and is subject to Anthropic's privacy policy

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for authentication (keeping you logged in), preferences (your chosen language and settings), and analytics (understanding how the site is used via anonymized data). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Anthropic (AI processing), Stripe (payment processing), Vercel (hosting), and Google Analytics (anonymized traffic analytics). Each of these services has their own privacy policy.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, email privacy@pdfmagic.io. We will respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account directly from Settings → Account → Delete Account.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@pdfmagic.io or write to: PDFMagic AI, HSR Layout, Bangalore, India 560102.

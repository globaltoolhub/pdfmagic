FAQ
Common questions
Is my PDF safe and private? +
Yes, absolutely. Your files are processed securely and automatically deleted from our servers within 1 hour. We never store, share, read, or train AI models on your documents. Your data is completely private.
What types of PDFs work best? +
PDFMagic works with research papers, academic articles, legal contracts, business reports, books, user manuals, financial documents, and any text-based PDF. Scanned image PDFs may have reduced accuracy.
How accurate is the AI summary? +
Our summaries are powered by Claude AI, one of the most capable language models available. Accuracy is very high for standard documents. We always recommend verifying critical information from the original source.
Can I use it for free? +
Yes! The free plan gives you 3 PDF summaries per day with no credit card required. For unlimited summaries and advanced features, upgrade to Pro for just $9.99/month.
Do you have an API for developers? +
Yes! Our Business plan includes full API access with 10,000 calls per month. You can integrate PDFMagic directly into your own applications. Contact our sales team for enterprise volumes.
